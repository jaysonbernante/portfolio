Project Overview

This system is about making gym operations easier and more organized by replacing manual processes with a digital solution. It allows the gym to manage members, track their membership duration, and record payments in one system. Instead of writing attendance manually, members use an RFID card to tap in when entering the gym, and their time is automatically recorded. It also helps the gym monitor who is active, expired, or about to expire, and keeps track of daily attendance and income. Overall, the system improves efficiency, reduces errors, and helps the gym staff manage everything faster and more accurately.

Lingunan Fitness Gym relied on manual processes for tracking attendance, memberships, and payments, which led to inefficiencies, time-consuming tasks, and possible record errors.
